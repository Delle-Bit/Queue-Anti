Privacy & Cookie Policy

Compliance with Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

1. Purpose & Scope

ReaLab Diagnostic Center values your privacy and is committed to protecting your sensitive medical and personal data. This policy explains how cookies and session identifiers are utilized across our queuing and patient portal platforms.

2. Cookie Categories

Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are necessary for core authentication, session validation, cross-site request forgery (CSRF) protection, and patient data security. These cannot be disabled.

Preference Cookies (Optional)

Store your customized UI settings such as dark/light theme choices and language preferences. Disabled by default unless explicitly accepted.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

Collect anonymized aggregate usage figures to improve queue performance and response times. No personally identifiable medical data is collected. Disabled by default.

3. Managing Your Consent

You can withdraw or update your consent preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of any page, or using the button below. Your choices are stored for 12 months.
